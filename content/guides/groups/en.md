# A party game for large groups

Most party games break down once the group gets big. Turns take forever, half the room is waiting and the quiet people never get a word in. Spy works better with more players, not worse: more clues means more suspects and more chances for the spy to slip up.

## How Spy works

Everyone gets the same secret word, except the spy, who gets a different one. Going around the circle, each player says one short clue about their word. Then the group talks it over and votes on who the spy is. The full rules are on the [how to play](/en/) page.

## Setting it up for 10 or more players

- **Everyone on their own phone.** With a big group, passing one phone around gets slow. Create an online room instead: everyone joins by scanning the QR code or opening the invite link. Nobody needs an account or an app.
- **More than one spy.** With 10 players you can have up to 4 spies, because spies are always fewer than half the players. Two or three spies is a good start.
- **Spies know each other, or not.** With "Spies know each other" turned on, the spies see who their partners are and can cover for each other. Leave it off and they have to find each other too.
- **Random number of spies.** Tap several numbers and each round picks one of them. Nobody knows how many spies there are, which makes the vote a lot more tense.
- **Keep clues short.** One word or a short phrase per player keeps a round of 15 people under ten minutes.
- **Use the timer.** A fixed discussion time stops the loudest people from talking forever.

## Which words work best

Words everyone knows work better in big groups, because there's always someone who doesn't know the rare ones. Good picks are [Food & Drink](/en/food/), [Animals](/en/animals/), [Places](/en/places/) and [Jobs & Roles](/en/jobs/). For mixed ages, leave out the harder categories.

## Good for

- birthday parties and house parties
- team events and office parties
- school classes and youth groups
- family gatherings over the holidays

Spy is free and runs in the browser, so a group can start playing within a minute. Start a room and share the link.
