# A Christmas party game for everyone at the table

After dinner, half the family is on the sofa and the other half is looking at their phones. Spy gets everyone playing in a minute: no board, no cards, no rules longer than a sentence. All you need is one phone and three or more people.

## How it works

Everyone gets the same secret word, say **Mulled wine**. Only the spy gets a different one, maybe **Hot chocolate**, or just a hint. Going around the table, everyone gives a short clue. Then you vote: who is the spy? The spy can still win by guessing the word. The full rules are on the [how to play](/en/) page.

## The Christmas word pack

The game has its own [Christmas & New Year category](/en/christmas/), from the Christmas tree and Secret Santa to mulled wine and sparklers. From December to Epiphany it's switched on by default, so you can just start playing. You can also mix it with other categories so not every round is about Christmas.

## Tips for playing with family

- **One phone is enough.** Pass it around the table. Everyone looks at their card and hands it on.
- **Pick the right hint for the group.** With kids or grandparents, use **Close hint**: the spy gets a word linked to the secret word and has a fair chance. Experienced players can try **Similar word**.
- **Use the timer** so the discussion doesn't go on longer than dessert.
- **Several spies for big families.** With 5 or more players you can have more than one spy.
- **No phones at the table?** Open the [Christmas word list](/en/christmas/), print it and write the pairs on slips of paper.
