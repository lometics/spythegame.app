# How to win at Spy

Spy is easy to learn: everyone gets the same secret word, except the spy, who gets a similar word, a hint or nothing at all. Winning is harder. These tips are for both sides. If you don't know the rules yet, read [how to play](/en/) first.

## Tips for civilians

Your clue has two jobs. It has to prove to the others that you know the word, and it must not give the word away to the spy. Say the word is **Bakery**:

- **Too obvious:** "Bread". The spy now knows exactly where you are.
- **Too vague:** "Building". Now you look like the spy yourself.
- **Just right:** "Early mornings" or "Flour on the floor". Anyone who knows the word gets it. The spy can only guess.

More things that help:

- Listen to the first clues before you settle on yours. If someone already said "Bread", don't stay in the same corner.
- Watch for clues that fit the category but not the word. A spy holding **Butcher** might say "Counter" or "Apron": true for a bakery, but just as true for a butcher.
- In the discussion, ask follow-up questions. A spy who is bluffing has to make up an answer on the spot.
- Don't vote too early. One strange clue can also come from a civilian who thought around the corner.

## Tips for the spy

You usually don't know you're the spy for sure when you get a similar word. Bakery and Butcher both feel like a normal word. So:

- If you can, give your clue late. The more clues you hear, the better you can tell whether your word fits.
- Keep your clue general enough to fit several words of the same kind. "You go there on Saturday morning" fits a bakery, a butcher and a market.
- Don't go too vague either. "Thing" or "Place" is the fastest way to get caught.
- If you get caught, you're not done yet. You win if you can guess the civilians' word, so pay attention to every clue.
- With **No hint**, you have nothing. Copy the mood of the other clues and stay calm. Confidence does half the work.

## Tips for the group

- Pick the hint level to match your group. New players do best with **Close hint**. Experienced groups should try **Similar word**, the hardest setting to catch.
- Use the discussion timer to keep rounds short. Five minutes is plenty for most groups.
- Try more than one spy with 5 or more players. Turn on "Spies know each other" for teamwork, or let each round pick a random number of spies so nobody knows how many there are.
- Swap categories often. [Food & Drink](/en/food/) plays very differently from [Mythology & Fantasy](/en/myth/).

Ready? Open the game on one phone or let everyone join on their own.
